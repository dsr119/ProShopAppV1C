Work Tickets on your MemoMind glasses

Your helpdesk tickets as a to-do list. Settings live here.

Outlook

Once, after saving the password: connect your work mailbox.

  • Look up or down to move, tap the glasses button to open a ticket, double tap to go back.
  • On a ticket: Check off, or hold the button and talk to reply, then tap when done. The reply goes on the ticket.
  • Refresh and Undo check-off are at the bottom of the list.
