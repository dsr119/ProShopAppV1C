EDITH on your MemoMind glasses

Settings live here; everything else happens on the glasses.

Starting…

  • Look up or down to move, tap the glasses button to open, double tap to go back.
  • Hold the button to talk to EDITH, and tap when you're done. Check what it heard, then tap to send.
  • Say "pull up the spec sheet for" a bowler's name. Tap for fingers, span, thumb; on the span, turn your head for Full, Cut to Cut, Cut to Outer.
