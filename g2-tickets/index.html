Work Tickets on your G2

Your helpdesk tickets as a to-do list. Settings live here.

Outlook

Once, after saving the password: connect your work mailbox.

  • Swipe to move, press to open a ticket, double press to go back or exit.
  • On a ticket: Check off, or hold and talk to reply. The reply goes on the ticket.
  • Tap then hold for the menu: Refresh, Undo check-off.
