Pro Shop on your G2

Settings live here; everything else happens on the glasses.

  • Swipe to move, press to open, double press to exit.
  • Hold to talk to EDITH. Release, check what it heard, press to send. Or turn on hands-free in the menu and just say "Hey EDITH".
  • Say "pull up the spec sheet for" a bowler's name. Press for fingers, span, thumb; on the span, swipe for Full, Cut to Cut, Cut to Outer.
  • Tap then hold for the menu: Talk to EDITH, Spec sheet, Start or stop hands-free, Refresh, Switch house.
